Beam-Deflection GUI

An interactive visualization I developed to help students see how beams deflect under load.

  • GUI Development
  • Visualization
  • Mechanics

Overview

I integrated the beam-deflection GUI into ENGR 151, Strength of Materials, so students could connect beam loading with the resulting deformation. I also developed and integrated a virtual teaching assistant (Vi-TA) to support students in the course.
