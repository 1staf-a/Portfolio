Interactive Beam Deflection

Developed an interactive tool to help students see how a load changes the shape of a beam.

  • GUI Development
  • Visualization
  • Mechanics

Classroom use

Integrated the beam-deflection interface into ENGR 151, Strength of Materials, so students could connect a loading condition with the resulting deformation. Also developed and integrated a virtual teaching assistant (Vi-TA) for the course.
