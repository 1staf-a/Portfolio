Modeling Ingress Losses in Gas Wells

Continuum and reduced-order modeling for subsurface gas transport in horizontal landfill gas wells, developed during research at Thompson Rivers University.

  • COMSOL
  • Java API
  • Reduced-Order Modeling
  • Porous Media Flow

Overview

This project examined pressure losses in horizontal landfill gas wells by comparing continuum modeling, reduced-order modeling, and quasi-1D approximation assumptions.
