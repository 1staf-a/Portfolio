Research report

Modeling Ingress Losses in Gas Wells

How much pressure is lost as landfill gas moves through porous waste and enters a horizontal collection well?

  • COMSOL
  • Java API
  • Reduced-Order Modeling

Overview

At Thompson Rivers University, I built a two-dimensional axisymmetric COMSOL model coupling porous-media flow with turbulent pipe flow. I used the COMSOL Java API to automate sweeps of variables such as outlet pressure, waste permeability, and aperture count, and to export data after each run.

I compared the COMSOL pressure profiles and collected gas mass with a quasi-one-dimensional model in MATLAB/GNU Octave. Pressure profiles were similar for the closed outer boundary, where both models collected the same gas mass. In the nominal closed-boundary case, the pressure at the closed end of the pipe stayed within about 500 Pa of the outlet pressure across the tested outlet pressures. With an open outer boundary, the models predicted different amounts of collected gas and different pressure profiles. I documented these limits in my internship report.
