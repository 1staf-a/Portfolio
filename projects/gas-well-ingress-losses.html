Research report

Pressure Losses in a Landfill Gas Well

Landfill gas moves through porous waste before entering a horizontal collection pipe. Compared two models to see how their predictions changed when gas could also enter from outside the waste layer.

  • COMSOL
  • Java API
  • Reduced-Order Modeling

Simulation and model comparison

Built a two-dimensional axisymmetric COMSOL model coupling Darcy flow through porous waste with turbulent pipe flow. Matched the pressures at the pipe openings to couple the two domains. Used the COMSOL Java API to automate sweeps over outlet pressure, waste permeability, and pipe aperture count. Compared pressure profiles and collected gas mass against a quasi-one-dimensional model implemented in MATLAB and GNU Octave.

Effect of the outer boundary

With no flow across the outer boundary of the waste layer, both models collected the same gas mass and predicted similar pipe pressure profiles. In the nominal closed-boundary case, pressure at the closed end of the pipe was at most about 500 Pa above the outlet pressure across the tested outlet pressures.

With the outer boundary held at atmospheric pressure, the models differed in both pipe pressure profiles and collected gas mass. The internship report documents these differences and identifies parameter combinations that produced unrealistic conditions or failed to converge.
