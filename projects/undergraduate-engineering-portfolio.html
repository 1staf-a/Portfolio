Undergraduate CFD and Aerodynamics

Selected undergraduate CFD projects involving unsteady flow, fluid–structure interaction, and experimental comparison.

  • CFD
  • Aerodynamics
  • Wind-Tunnel Testing

Overview

I used ANSYS Fluent and a user-defined function to simulate a heaving airfoil and investigate how vortex shedding affects power generation. In a separate wing study, I used two-way fluid–structure interaction modeling to examine how truss bracing affected flow-induced vibration and to identify high-stress regions.

Wind-tunnel comparison

I also compared a Fluent prediction of a model vehicle’s drag coefficient with a coefficient calculated from wind-tunnel pressure measurements.
