Simulation and wind-tunnel testing

Undergraduate Aerodynamics Projects

Investigated how airflow affects a moving airfoil and a braced wing, and checked a vehicle drag prediction against wind-tunnel data.

  • CFD
  • Aerodynamics
  • Wind-Tunnel Testing

Heaving airfoil

Simulated turbulent flow around a heaving airfoil in ANSYS Fluent using a user-defined function. Examined how the vortices shed by the oscillating airfoil affect aerodynamic forces and the potential for power generation.

Truss-braced wing

Modeled a subsonic wing using two-way fluid–structure interaction in ANSYS Fluent. Examined how truss bracing affected flow-induced vibration and identified high-stress regions.

Wind-tunnel comparison

Compared the drag coefficient predicted in Fluent for a model vehicle with a coefficient calculated from wind-tunnel pressure measurements.
