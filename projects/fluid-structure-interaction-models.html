Flexible Filaments in Flow

A fluid can bend a flexible filament, and the filament’s changing shape can alter the flow. This work explores a simpler way to model that two-way interaction for slender biological filaments.

  • Fluid–Structure Interaction
  • Reduced-Order Modeling
  • Computational Mechanics

Variational formulation

Led early development of a variational formulation for slender filaments undergoing large deformations. The modeling objective was to retain coupling between filament deformation and surrounding flow in a reduced-order description for low to moderate Reynolds numbers.
