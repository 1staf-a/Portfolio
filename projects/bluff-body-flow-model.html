Wind Loads on a Square Cylinder

Wind can exert changing forces on a building even when the incoming flow is steady. This study uses a square cylinder as a simplified building shape to explore those fluctuations.

  • MATLAB
  • Aerodynamics
  • Wind–Structure Interaction

Flow model

Developed a two-dimensional MATLAB model of flow around a square cylinder and examined how the aerodynamic forces on the cylinder oscillate over time.
