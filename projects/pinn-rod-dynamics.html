Ongoing research

Predicting How Flexible Rods Move

How does a flexible rod bend and twist, and what can its motion reveal about how it resists bending? This research uses neural networks that learn from simulated motion while accounting for the laws of mechanics.

  • Physics-Informed Modeling
  • Rod Dynamics
  • Python / PyTorch

Forward and inverse rod models

Implemented the lab’s MATLAB nonlinear rod formulation in Python and PyTorch. Developed physics-informed neural networks for static and three-dimensional dynamic rod problems. The forward model predicts the rod’s response for a prescribed material law. The inverse model estimates the relationship between bending moment and curvature, predicting the two quantities as independent fields.

Used automatic differentiation to compute derivatives along the rod and, in dynamic cases, over time. Defined separate loss terms for the governing equations, reference data, boundary conditions, and initial conditions where applicable.

Training and evaluation

Investigate slow convergence when training on data and physics constraints together. Experiments include pretraining on reference data, Fourier features, Adam optimization, learning-rate adaptation, and variable loss weights.

Compare predicted fields with analytical or simulated reference solutions and track individual loss terms to assess accuracy, numerical scaling, and convergence in selected test cases. Reliable recovery of the nonlinear moment–curvature law remains an open objective.
