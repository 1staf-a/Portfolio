Research

My research with the Goyal Research Group explores how flexible structures deform under loads and how they interact with surrounding fluids. The group applies mechanics and controls to problems in biology and medicine.

Get in touch about research collaborations.

Learning from rod motion

Study how a rod’s motion changes under load and what that motion can reveal about the way it bends.

Large deflections in beams and rods

When a beam bends substantially, its new shape affects how it carries the load. A Python solver models this response for a beam fixed at one end.

Fluid–structure interaction

A flowing fluid can bend a thin filament, and the change in shape can alter the flow. This work explores how to capture that interaction in a simpler model.
