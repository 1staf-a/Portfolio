About

Research-oriented engineering and scientific computing.

I am a Mechanical Engineering PhD student at the University of California, Merced, working across physics-based computational modeling of structural, fluid, and coupled engineering systems. My current interests include nonlinear rod dynamics, variational mechanics, linear controls, fluid-structure interaction, numerical methods, and research software development.

My background combines graduate research, teaching, CFD design engineering, and undergraduate research in mechanical engineering. I am especially interested in simulation workflows that connect mathematical models, numerical solvers, validation, visualization, and engineering interpretation.
